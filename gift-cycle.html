The Outward Half

Offerings

An Intention faces inward. An Offering faces out.

An Offering is something you hold out for someone else — bound to no intention, asking for nothing in return. Peers who want it bid a little of the time they've accrued, and you, the steward, choose who receives it. It wears a rose tag.

The tool runs on this quiet exchange — intentions named, manifestations made, offerings held out and received. Attention given freely, and returned.
