01 · Arrive

You arrive as a peer

Some of the people on your wish list you've only ever known as sigils and signatures. Then you walk into a Temple courtyard and one of them hands you a bowl of soup. Gatherings of the Tribe are hosted at Temples around the world — the same circle, in person.

02 · Ceremony

Co-created, night by night

There is no fixed program. Ceremony, celebration, music, work on the land — however the community decides to come together. The way intentions gather attention in the game, the gathering itself takes the shape of what the circle cares about.

03 · The Bazaar

The Bazaar opens

At the close of every Gathering, a marketplace emerges. The wish list walks off the screen: a jar of honey, bodywork for tired shoulders, a cheesecake carrying 4h 40m of collective attention — intentions fulfilled at real tables, between real hands.

04 · Gratitude

Gratitude flows onward

Gratitude worth each intention's accumulated attention releases to the doer — recorded, and signed by both — in the same ledger the game runs on. Tagged onward to the next wish, it keeps the loop turning long after everyone has gone home.

GratiDAO Principles

GratiDAO—Portuguese for Gratitude—represents our organizational philosophy: Gratitude-based, Decentralized, Distributed, Autonomous, and Alive.

  • The One Commandment: Recognize the divine in every Other and treat them as an extension of yourself. Membership is affirming this — and sealing it with an offering.
  • A founding gift, once: Your offering is your signature on the Covenant. It rests on your profile, read by every peer as proof of membership — for life.
  • Temple Network Access: Land-based sanctuaries around the world where the community gathers.
  • The Right to Fork: Written into the Bylaws — the Covenant guarantees non-extraction, attention as gift, and the community's right to fork the codebase.
