A wish list you share

It begins as a big wish list, shared with the people you trust. Each wish is an intention — something you want to bring into reality, named out loud so it's real and visible instead of a vague someday.

Everyone votes with attention

There's nothing to buy and nothing to game. The only vote is attention — the time each person freely gives to the wishes that matter most to them. Watch where it gathers, and you can see what your circle truly cares about.

Attention becomes incentive

As an intention accumulates collective attention, it begins to glow. Sooner or later the pull becomes irresistible — someone steps in to manifest it, and make the thing real.

Proof, then gratitude

The manifester shares proof of their service, and everyone is notified. Gratitude worth the intention's accumulated attention releases to the doer — recorded, and signed by both.

The cycle continues

The doer tags that gratitude onto whatever they want to see done next. Attention becomes manifestation becomes gratitude becomes attention — and with every turn of the loop, the world gets a little more beautiful.

Intentions become manifestations — through collective attention.

Built on Indra's Network

A sovereign peer-to-peer stack. Servers are optional: every player's device holds a full encrypted copy, and peers connect directly to each other. Your content is shared only with those you explicitly allow. To everyone else, it is post-quantum encrypted noise.

The mesh self-organizes — no central coordinator required

If you're offline, mutual contacts carry sealed packets they can't read and deliver them when you return. The network routes through relationships, not data centers — as long as peers keep running, it exists.

Built with: Rust · Iroh (QUIC P2P) · Automerge CRDTs · BLAKE3 · Post-quantum encryption. No blockchain. No cloud.
