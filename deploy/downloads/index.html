macOS

.dmg · Apple Silicon (M-series)

Get for Mac — Apple Silicon

Apple Silicon = any Mac from 2020 on ( → About This Mac shows "Chip: Apple M…"). An Intel build is coming soon.

Open the .dmg and drag SyncEngine to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it "could not verify" the app — it isn't notarized by Apple yet. Approve it once in System Settings → Privacy & Security: scroll down and click Open Anyway, then confirm.

Android

.apk · arm64

Get for Android

Allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser, then open the .apk. It's release-signed, so future versions install over the top.

The app is yours to use privately, without joining anything. No account, no server — it lives on your device.
